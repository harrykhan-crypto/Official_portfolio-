Hi, I’m Hasnain Maqsood — I design things people don’t have to think about.

I’m a UI/UX designer who starts with the user’s problem, not the screen. I run quick research, map the flow, then build clean, accessible interfaces and hand them off in a form developers actually enjoy working with.

When I’m not in Figma you’ll find me testing prototypes on friends, reading about behavioural design or tweaking a layout that was already fine.
